Walkthrough

The whole app in about a minute and a half.

Start a challenge

The first launch walks you through it in four steps.

  1. 1Pick a pathStart from a built-in program (75 Days, 75 Soft, Core 4 and more) or write your own.
  2. 2Set it upName it, pick a length (7–365 days) and add your daily tasks.
  3. 3Choose a modeStrict asks what to do when you miss a day. Flexible logs the miss and keeps going.
  4. 4Review & startCheck the dates, or tap Start on a different day to schedule it. Then turn on a daily reminder.

Quit halfway? Relaunch and your draft comes back at the Review step. Re-check the mode before you continue.

Home

Your challenges and this week's progress, at a glance.

  1. 1New challenge. Opens the program list, or build your own.
  2. 2Menu. App Settings and Help & Support.
  3. 3Challenge cards. Tap to open that challenge's tasks. Long-press for Open, Settings or Delete.
  4. 4This Week. Tap a day to see what you did.
  5. 5Streak & stats. Tap to open Statistics.

Scroll down for Your Journey (recent photos, videos and voice notes) and any upcoming or paused challenges.

Today's tasks

The Challenge tab is your daily checklist.

  1. 1Challenge settings. Name, dates, mode, tasks.
  2. 2Switch challenge. Only shows when you have more than one active.
  3. 3Where you are. Day count, days left and your streak.
  4. 4Water & counters. Tap + to log an amount. Done automatically at the target.
  5. 5Photo, video, voice. Tap the button to capture. Saving checks the task off.
  6. 6Steps. Synced from Apple Health.
  7. 7Everything else. Tap the circle to check it off. Tap again to undo.

Handy gestures

  • Swipe a task left to set a reminder for it, edit it or remove it.
  • Long-press a task for more options, including Manage Tasks to add, reorder or delete tasks.
  • Scroll to Notes at the bottom to write about the day.

Task types

TypeHow you complete it
StandardTap the circle.
WaterLog ounces or millilitres. Quick-add buttons for common amounts.
CounterLog pages, reps, minutes or anything else you count.
StepsReads your step count from Apple Health. The app never writes to Health.
Photo / VideoTake a photo or a video up to 60 seconds, or pick one from your library.
Voice noteRecord audio, with no length limit.

Calendar

Every day of the challenge on one screen.

  1. 1Gallery. All of this challenge's photos, videos and voice notes.
  2. 2Green means every task was done that day.
  3. 3Blue is today.
  4. 4Future days stay empty until they arrive.

Tap any past day to see its tasks, notes and media. Past days are read-only, so you can't check off a task after the day has ended.

Stats

Open from the streak card on Home.

  1. 1Completion rate across the selected range.
  2. 2Totals: tasks done, active days and your best streak.
  3. 3Range: week, month, 3 months, year or all time.
  4. 4Trend. Tap a bar to see that day.

Photos, video & voice notes

  • Capture from a photo, video or voice-note task on the Challenge tab. The first time, iOS asks for camera or microphone access.
  • Browse everything in the gallery: Home → Your Journey → See All, or the gallery icon on the Calendar tab. Filter by challenge, day or type.
  • Save or share from the ⋯ menu when viewing an item. Long-press a thumbnail to do it from the grid.
  • Copy to your Photos app automatically: turn on Settings → Media → Save Photos / Videos to Phone.

Deleting media can't be undone. There is no trash folder.

Managing challenges

Open a challenge's settings with the ⚙ on the Challenge or Calendar tab.

  • Edit the name, start date, length, the time your day ends, Strict/Flexible mode and the task list.
  • Start New Attempt restarts from Day 1 and clears your check-offs. The challenge and its tasks stay.
  • Duplicate, Complete early or Delete are at the bottom.
  • Run more than one at a time: tap + on Home. Switch between them with ⇄.
  • Schedule one for later by giving it a future start date. It starts on its own that day.

When the last day passes, you get a summary with options to Start Again or Extend by 7 days, 30 days or the original length.

Reminders & widgets

Reminders

  • Day-end reminder: challenge settings → Notifications. It fires 1, 2 or 3 hours before your day ends.
  • Per-task reminder: swipe a task left → Remind. Pick the days and time.
  • If nothing arrives, check iOS Settings → Notifications → FlexChallenge.

Widgets

  • Add one: long-press your Home Screen → + → FlexChallenge. It comes in small, medium and large.
  • Check off tasks and log water or counts right from the widget. Steps tasks can only be viewed there.
  • Pick which challenge it shows: challenge settings → Widget → Show in Widget.

Subscription, backup & settings

App settings live under Home → ⋯ → Settings.

  • Manage or cancel your plan from Settings → Subscription → Manage Subscription. Apple handles billing.
  • New phone or reinstall? Tap Restore Purchases. You won't be charged twice.
  • Back up from Settings → Data → Backup & Restore → Export Backup. Save the file to Files or iCloud Drive.
  • Theme, haptics and confetti can be toggled in Settings too.

Importing a backup replaces everything on the device, so export a fresh one first.

Backups don't include photos, videos or voice notes. They hold your challenges, tasks and check-offs.

Troubleshooting

My day didn't roll over until I opened the app

That's by design. The new day starts when you open the app after your day-end time. Opening it also resets counters and starts any scheduled challenge.

Strict mode stopped me when I opened the app

You missed tasks yesterday. Choose Mark All Complete & Continue, Continue Without Marking or Start Challenge Over. A restart always asks you to confirm twice.

My steps aren't updating

Allow Health access in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → FlexChallenge, then tap the sync button on the steps task.

A task checked or unchecked itself

Water, counter and steps tasks complete when they reach the target, and uncheck if the number drops below it. If you check one off by hand, it stays checked.

My challenge says complete, but I wasn't done

A challenge ends when its last day passes, whatever your score. Use Extend on the summary to add days, or Start Again.

"Start Now" turned off my other challenges

Starting a scheduled challenge early pauses your other active ones. To turn them back on, open ⇄, swipe the challenge right and tap Activate.

My subscription ended. Did I lose everything?

No. Nothing is deleted. Your challenges, history and media come back as soon as you resubscribe or restore. Your data lives only on your phone, so export a backup if you're leaving.

The widget is behind

iOS refreshes widgets about every 15 minutes. Open the app to refresh it right away.

Still stuck?

Email support@flexchallenge.app with your iOS version and what happened. We read everything.

In the app: Home → ⋯ → Help & Support → Contact Support.
