THE METHOD
01 / 06

Why challenges work
when streaks don't.

№1

You choose it daily

Showing up is a decision you make every morning. That's where discipline comes from. A streak you're scared to break doesn't build much.

№2

A real end date

Seven days or 365, you pick. When you know where it ends, it's easier to commit, and you actually get to finish.

№3

Finish, then stack

When one challenge ends, start the next. Some of it sticks as habit. The rest becomes a harder challenge.
