The premise
01 / 06

Forever is not a commitment.
It's a wish.

Habit apps ask you to be consistent indefinitely, then punish you with a broken number when life happens. That's not accountability. It's anxiety with a counter attached.

Streak

Open-ended. The only outcome is eventually failing. Miss once at day 200 and the app tells you you're back at zero — which was never true.

Challenge

Bounded. Day 1, a final day, and a fixed set of terms you agreed to in advance. You either finish or you don't — and finishing is a thing you can actually do.

On integrity

Nobody is watching.
That's the whole point.

There is no leaderboard here, no followers, no streak to post. The only person who will ever know whether day 41 was honest is you — which is exactly the muscle worth building. Do the work when it's inconvenient, log it truthfully when it went badly, and on the last day you'll own something that can't be bought or faked.
